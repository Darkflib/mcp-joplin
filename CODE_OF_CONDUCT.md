# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in Darkflib/mcp-joplin, in issues, pull requests, reviews and any
other space it uses, is expected to:

- be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- accept that others have different experience and points of view;
- assume good faith, and ask before concluding the worst.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks,
unwanted sexual attention, publishing someone's private information, and
sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block anyone
whose behaviour breaks this code. To report a problem privately, use the
contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and say that it's about conduct.
